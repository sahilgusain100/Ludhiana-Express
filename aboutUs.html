Who We Are ?

Ludhiana Express is a trusted laundry service dedicated to providing high-quality garment care. We help customers save time by offering professional washing, cleaning, and ironing services with attention to detail and customer satisfaction.

Our Journey

What started as a small local laundry service has grown into a reliable solution for individuals and families looking for convenient and affordable garment care. Our commitment to quality and punctuality has helped us build lasting relationships with our customers.
